# Week 7
In the next part of this course, we're going to look more heavily at routing. In particular, we need to understand dynamic routing as it would be performed on LANs at all scales. And for the first time we need to consider the topologies of larger networks with greater complexity. And finally the architecture of our largest network, the Internet.

## Study
The simplest dynamic routing protocol we will consider is [RIP](https://johnoraw.gitbook.io/routing-theory/routing-information-protocol-rip). It has a limited number of use cases these days, but we will also examine where it is an appropriate protocol for us to use. In contrast, we will also learn about [OSPF](https://johnoraw.gitbook.io/routing-theory/ospf), a protocol we could use for very large enterprises and for enterprise data centres.

## Practice
In our exercises this week, we will utilize with both [RIP](https://johnoraw.gitbook.io/routing-practice/rip) and [OSPF](https://johnoraw.gitbook.io/routing-practice/ospf-1). Please make sure you understand where it is appropriate to use each. The assignments in the second half of this module normally require a competence with OSPF.
