# Week 8
The largest network that we use is the Internet. At time of writing, it remains wholly reliant on the border gateway protocol (BGPv4). This week we want to gain an understanding of how the Internet works. And in data centres once we get to cloud scale and the biggest vendors, a type of internal BGP (iBGP) is also used almost exclusively.
This week’s notes and exercises finish the first part of the course, on routing.

## Study
Firstly, go through the theory on [BGP](https://johnoraw.gitbook.io/routing-theory/bgp). You should be beginning to understand how the Internet works as you complete this.

## Practice
I get you to do some simple [exercises in BGP](https://johnoraw.gitbook.io/routing-practice/bgp-1) this week. Be aware, what I am covering is trivial. In the professional world, this is the domain of the large carrier and the most expert skills I know in networking.
