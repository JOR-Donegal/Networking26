Week 3

Over the next few weeks, we will study the design of Local Area Networks (LANs). Historically, we had many possible protocols at Layer 2, modern LANs are almost entirely based on Ethernet. At Layer 3/4, we also had many possible solutions, which have rationalized down to TCP/IP. We will begin this week by diving into Ethernet. We will also do practical exercises to better understand how Ethernet works.

Study

  1. Begin by reading my introduction to Ethernet. Unless you understand where it came from, the terminology and operation of modern Ethernet will never make sense.
  2. As the technology developed, we utilized four families of Ethernet devices. You need to understand what they were and their operation principles.
  3. One of the problems we may have on an Ethernet is a network loop. We need to understand how to implement loop avoidance.

Practical Work

This week, carry out these exercises.

  1. Its time to start building switched networks.
  2. You must know how to correctly configure loop avoidance.
