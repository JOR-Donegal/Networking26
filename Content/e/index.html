Week 5

This week, we're going to address layer 3 and routing. For the moment, I'm going to concentrate on IPv4 and you will find subnetting a challenge. This doesn't require mathematical skill, it is algorithmic and it requires practice and experience.

Study

  1. First, I introduce layer 3.
  2. The next topic is to understand subnetting with IPv4. I will do a live tutorial on this.

Practical Work

This week, carry out these exercises.

  1. To keep with the core requirements of the module, we are going to play with an OS running in Docker and do some simple scripting on network hardware.
  2. I need you to know how to do simple routing on a LAN with L2/L3 switches.
  3. There is a variation on LAN design, on sites which have a perimeter router, we call this router-on-a-stick.

Later Study

If you run short on time now...these are items you need to know, but you do not need for the assignments.

At the moment, I'm not doing exercises on IPv6 theory in this module, but you need to understand what it is!
